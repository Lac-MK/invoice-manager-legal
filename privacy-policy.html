Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Zero Data Collection and Telemetry

The App is designed to operate offline without requiring internet access for its core functionality.

No Developer Data Collection: The developer does not collect users' invoice numbers, client details, hourly rates, expense amounts, or other financial records.

No Telemetry: The App does not intentionally transmit usage analytics, diagnostic reports, or crash logs to the developer.

2. Local Device Storage Only

All input data and generated reports are stored locally within the application's device storage.

The App does not automatically upload financial information to external servers.

Users may explicitly export, back up, or share files using supported device functionality.

Deleting the App normally removes its locally stored application data, subject to operating system backup and storage behavior.

3. Third-Party Libraries

The App does not intentionally include advertising networks, tracking scripts, or cloud analytics SDKs that collect user statistics. It is built entirely on Apple's first-party frameworks (SwiftUI, Foundation, UIKit, PDFKit, PhotosUI, Vision) with no third-party dependencies.

Questions about this policy can be sent to benkhelilmehdi@hotmail.com.
