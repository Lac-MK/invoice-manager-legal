Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
All your data (activities, expenses, invoices, receipts) is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

How do I back up my data?
Go to Settings → Export & Backup → Export Full Backup, and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox. Use Import Backup on the same screen to restore it.
